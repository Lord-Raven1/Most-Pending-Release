# Privacy

_Last updated: 13 October 2026_

**Short version: Most-Pending doesn't collect your data.** Everything you put
in it stays on your PC. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and
no ads.

## What stays on your PC

Your tasks, work sessions, routines, shopping list, reminders, subscriptions,
focus history and settings are saved in `%AppData%\Most-Pending\` (with
automatic backups in its `backups` folder). They are never sent anywhere.

The files aren't encrypted, so anyone who can use your Windows account can
read them. The same goes for any file you create with **Settings > Export All
Data**: it's yours to keep safe.

## When the app uses the internet

- **Update checks.** Shortly after starting, every few hours, and when you
  click **Check for Updates**, the app asks GitHub
  ([github.com](https://github.com)) whether there's a newer version, and
  downloads it if there is. Like any web request, this lets GitHub see your
  IP address. None of your data is sent. See
  [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Send Feedback.** This opens your web browser on a pre-filled GitHub issue.
  Nothing is sent until you submit it there. You choose whether to include
  your app and Windows version. Submitting needs a GitHub account, and
  **issues are public**, so please don't include anything personal.

That's all the app sends or receives.

## Notifications

Reminders are shown by Windows on your PC. They don't go through any server.
